Privacy notice

Last updated 15 September 2026

In short: your sales, stock, prices, customers and staff stay on your store's own computer or tablet. This website keeps only what it needs to link your devices to your store and to know until when your subscription is paid.

Who we are

Chachi POS is made by Chachi's Software Development Service, Koronadal City, South Cotabato, Philippines. Write to us at ChachiSoftware@gmail.com about anything in this notice.

What stays in your store

Chachi POS runs on your own device and keeps its records there: sales, receipts, stock, prices, customers and their credit, suppliers, shifts, users and the audit trail. None of it is sent to us, and we have no copy of it. Your backups are saved on your device or in a folder you choose.

What this website keeps

WhatWhenWhy
Your Google account ID, email address and name When the store owner signs in with Google at /link To know who owns the store. Only the owner signs in; your name is kept for the one-hour sign-in and not stored with the store
The store's name, its plan and the date it is paid until When a store is created To run the subscription
For each linked device: a random device ID, the kind of device (for example Windows or Android), the app version, when it was linked and when it last checked in When a device is linked, and at each subscription check To list your devices and renew each one's licence. The device's renewal secret is stored only as a one-way hash
Payments: the amount, a reference and a note; for Google Play, the purchase token, the product and its expiry When a payment is recorded To extend the subscription and answer questions about a payment
Standard web server logs: IP address, time and the page requested On every visit To keep the service secure and working

The subscription check sends only the store's name, the app version, the device ID and its renewal secret. It never sends sales, stock or customer data.

Cookies

The pages you are reading set no cookies. Linking a device sets one sign-in cookie that expires after an hour. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts.

Who else is involved

  • Google, when you choose to sign in with Google, and to confirm Google Play purchases.
  • Our hosting, on a server we run for Chachi's Software.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

For as long as your store has an account with us. A device you remove stops being able to renew. To have your store's record removed entirely, email us.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you can ask to see the information we keep about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Email ChachiSoftware@gmail.com and we will answer.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
