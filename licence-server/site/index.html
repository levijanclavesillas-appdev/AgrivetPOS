What it does

Everything from the first sale to the last peso in the drawer

These are the real screens, from a demo pharmacy's products.

A counter that keeps up with the line

Scan the barcode or type a few letters. A busy moment can be parked while you serve the next customer, and the cart is still there after a power cut.

  • Cash, GCash, QR Ph, utang and store creditSplit one sale across several, with the change worked out for you.
  • Receipts on a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printerOver USB or the network, and the cash drawer opens on cash sales.
  • No sale is ever edited or deletedReturns and voids are separate records, with who approved them.

Know what's on the shelf, and what's about to expire

Everything that comes in, goes out, is counted or is written off is recorded. The stock figure always has a reason behind it.

  • Batches and expiryFirst-expiring stock sells first, expired stock is blocked, and a batch can be recalled in one step.
  • Low-stock alerts and stock countsCount the shelf, see the difference, post the adjustment.
  • Purchase orders and deliveriesReceive against the order; the average cost updates as stock arrives.

Utang, cash and the day's numbers, settled

Credit customers get limits and terms, and every peso in the drawer is accounted for at closing.

  • Credit limits, statements and ageingPayments settle the oldest debt first. Print a statement for any customer.
  • A shift close that countsExpected against counted, for each way of paying, with a reason for any difference.
  • Reports you will actually readDaily sales, gross profit, top and slow sellers and GCash reconciliation, with CSV export.

Questions

What store owners ask us

How to do something in the POS? It's in the user guide.

Is there a separate app for my kind of store?

No. There is one app, Chachi POS. When you set it up, you choose what kind of store it is (a pharmacy or an agrivet supply store, with motorcycle shops and wholesale & retail coming), and it starts with the settings that kind of store needs. The sign-in screen shows it, for example Chachi POS (Pharmacy).

Do I need the internet to use it?

Not to sell. Everything runs on your own PC or tablet. When it's online, the POS checks your subscription in the background, and it keeps working for 30 days without a connection, plus 7 days' grace with a warning.

Can you see my sales?

No. Your sales, stock and customer records never leave your device. The subscription check sends only your store's name, the app version and a device ID. See our privacy notice.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your records stay yours, on your device. When a subscription runs out, a new shift can't be opened, but reports, export, backups and restore keep working.

Does it print official receipts?

No. Chachi POS prints sales receipts marked "This is not an official receipt". It isn't BIR-accredited, so keep issuing your official receipts or invoices as you do today.

Can a pharmacy use it for prescription medicines?

Chachi POS for pharmacies is made for over-the-counter drugstores. It doesn't keep a prescription book or a dangerous-drugs register, so it isn't meant for dispensing prescription drugs.

Which printers and scanners work?

Thermal receipt printers that use ESC/POS, 58 mm or 80 mm, over USB or the network, and USB barcode scanners. A cash drawer connects through the printer. On Android, use a network (Wi-Fi or LAN) printer. Bluetooth printers aren't supported yet.

Is the Android app on Google Play?

Not yet. We install it on your tablet or phone for you.

I already have Chachi POS. Where do I link it?

On the POS, open Admin → Subscription → Link this POS to get a code, then enter it at pos.chachisoftware.store/link. Only the owner signs in with Google. Everyone at the counter keeps their own POS login.
