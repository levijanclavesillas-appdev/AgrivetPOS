Chachi POS user guide

How to set up Chachi POS, sell at the counter, look after your stock and your customers' utang, and close the day. The guide follows the Pharmacy edition; where the Agrivet edition or the Android app works differently, it says so.

New to Chachi POS?

Read these three first, in order: Getting started (install, set up, sign in), Products and prices (load your shelf), and Selling at the counter (your first sale). A cashier only needs the third one and Cash and closing the shift.
