What you need

Chachi POS runs on one computer or tablet in the store, and keeps everything on it. You do not need the internet to sell, print or back up.

Install on a Windows PC

Who: usually Chachi's, on installation day

Chachi POS comes as one installer file, ChachiPharmacyPOS-Setup- plus the version number. It needs no internet and no administrator password.

  1. Copy the installer from the USB stick to the PC and double-click it.
  2. Keep the suggested install folder, or choose another, and let it install.
  3. When it finishes, Chachi POS starts and opens the setup wizard (below).

A Chachi Pharmacy POS shortcut is added to the desktop and the Start menu. The store's data is kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ChachiPharmacyPOS.

Important: if Windows asks for an administrator password while installing, stop and call Chachi's. If Windows warns that the file is unrecognised, continue only if it came to you directly from Chachi's.

Updates never install themselves. A new version comes as a new installer: close every shift, quit the app, and run it over the old one in the same folder. It backs up your data before changing anything. Uninstalling removes the program only; the database and backup folder stay, and reinstalling finds them.

Install on an Android tablet or phone

Who: Chachi's installs the app for you

The Android app is not on Google Play; Chachi's installs it. It is the same program as on Windows, with the same screens and rules.

  1. Open Chachi Pharmacy POS. The first time, it asks Where should backups go?
  2. Tap Allow. On the next screen turn on Allow access to manage all files, then come back.
  3. The setup wizard opens. It is the same as on Windows (below).

Backups then go to Documents/ChachiPharmacyPOS Backups, which stays if the app is removed and can be copied to a computer by USB cable.

Important: if you tap Not now, backups stay inside the app and are deleted if it is uninstalled. The whole store lives on the tablet, so losing it without a backup copied off loses the store.

On Android: finish the setup wizard on the day the app is installed, before installing other apps. Use a network receipt printer. Downloaded files, such as the Excel template or an export, go to Downloads.

Run the setup wizard

Who: the store owner, usually with Chachi's

The wizard runs once, the first time Chachi POS starts. Nothing is saved until you press Finish setup; if the app is closed halfway, it starts again at step 1. Use Next and Back to move between steps.

  1. 1 · Your store. Type the Store name, Address, Contact number and, if you have one, the TIN. They print at the top of every receipt.
  2. 2 · Tax. Choose Not registered (prices are final), Non-VAT (percentage tax) (prices are final, no tax shown on the receipt) or VAT-registered (prices include 12% VAT, and receipts show a VAT summary). Ask your accountant if unsure. Only the owner can change it later.
  3. 3 · Owner account. Type the owner's Full name and a Username (3 to 32 characters). Choose a Password of at least 10 characters and repeat it in Confirm password. The Counter PIN (6 digits) is optional.
  4. 4 · Recovery code. Read the note and tick I understand the code is shown once and I am ready to write it down. Have paper and a pen ready.
  5. 5 · Backup folder. Keep the suggested folder (on Windows, Documents\ChachiPharmacyPOS Backups) or type the full path of another, ideally on a second drive. Press Finish setup.
  6. Setup complete shows your recovery code, such as NQ85-YP3L-U8H2-CFU6. Write it down now; it is never shown again.
  7. Press I have written it down — next to load your products now, or Skip — go to the application.

Step 6 · Your data (optional)

Step 6 loads the products, opening stock and customer balances you already have from one Excel workbook: Download the Excel template, fill it in, choose it, press Check, then Load this data, and finally Go to the application. You can also do it later in Admin → Export / import. Details are in Products and prices.

Keep the recovery code safe

Who: the store owner

Chachi POS works offline, so there is no "forgot password" email. The recovery code is the only way back into the owner account if the owner password is lost. The app keeps only a scrambled copy that cannot be read back, so neither the app nor Chachi's can show it to you again.

  1. Write the code on paper exactly as shown, with the dashes.
  2. Keep it with the business permits or the cash box, not in the drawer beside the computer.
  3. Do not photograph it onto a phone that other people use.

Important: if the owner password is forgotten and the code is lost too, nobody can get into the owner account. If you have lost the code, tell Chachi's now, while you can still sign in. See also Troubleshooting.

Link your subscription and start the free trial

Who: Owner, with the Google account that owns the store

Until the POS is linked, no shift can be opened, so nothing can be sold. Linking needs the internet once. You sign in with Google on a web page, not in the POS, and the POS never sees your Google password. Everyone else keeps signing in with their own POS username.

  1. Sign in as the owner and open Admin, then the Subscription tab. It says Not linked.
  2. Press Link this POS. A code such as K7QM-3XPD appears under Approve it from any browser. It works for 15 minutes.
  3. Press Open the link page. Or, on your phone, go to pos.chachisoftware.store/link, type the code and press Continue.
  4. Press Continue with Google and sign in with the store owner's Google account.
  5. On Approve this device?, check the code matches. For your first device, check the Store name. For another device of the same store, choose your store. Press Approve.
  6. The page says Linked. Within a few seconds the POS shows Active and "Subscribed until" a date.

A new store starts on a 14-day free trial, shown as Paid until. To continue after it, contact Chachi's to pay by GCash or bank transfer; the POS picks up the new date at its next check. It checks by itself whenever it has the internet, and must get online at least once every 30 days. The warnings are explained in The subscription screen and its warnings.

Sign in

Who: everyone

  1. Type your Username and Password.
  2. Press Sign in or Enter.

A Cashier lands on POS, an Inventory clerk on Products, and a Manager or Owner on the Reports dashboard. To sell, open your shift first (Selling at the counter).

Incorrect username or password is shown for either mistake. Check Caps Lock and try again. After 5 wrong tries in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes (This account is locked for … more minutes…). Wait, or ask the owner to set a new password in Admin → Users, which unlocks it at once (Reset a password or PIN). Staff cannot change their own password.

When the screen locks, and your PIN

Who: everyone

After 15 minutes without use (the owner can change this in Admin → Settings), your session ends. The screen locks the next time someone clicks or scans. The cart is not lost.

  1. If your shift is open, you see Screen locked and Your cart is kept.
  2. Type your 6-digit PIN and press Unlock. You are back where you were.

A PIN only unlocks your own open shift; it is not a way to sign in. If your shift is closed, the sign-in screen appears with your username filled in and A PIN unlocks an open shift. Sign in with your password to open one. If you have no PIN, press Different user and sign in with your password.

A PIN unlock is for carrying on with the sale in front of you. It never opens Settings, Users, product editing or reports. If you see A PIN session cannot … Sign in with your password., click your username at the bottom of the menu, press Different user, and sign in with your password. Your cart is kept.

Important: wrong PINs count toward the same 5-try lock as wrong passwords. Do not guess.

Lock the screen, sign out or change user

Who: everyone

There is no separate sign-out button. Use your username at the bottom of the menu whenever you leave the counter or hand over.

  1. Click your username at the bottom of the menu. If you have a PIN, the screen locks and you can come back with it.
  2. To hand over, press Different user. The next person signs in. The previous cashier's cart is kept on their shift. means nothing was thrown away.

Without a PIN, clicking your username goes straight to the sign-in screen. Closing the app also signs you out.

Tip: never let someone sell under your name. Every sale, discount and drawer count is recorded against whoever is signed in.
