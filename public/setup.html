Chachi POS

Set up your store

Five steps. Nothing is saved until the last one.

    Your sales, stock and customers stay on this computer. The subscription is checked online once a month; selling never needs the internet.

    Moving from another computer? Restore its backup instead of setting up a new store.

    Your store is already on the web? Connect this device to it instead.

    1 · Your store

    What kind of store is this? *

    This sets the defaults a store like yours starts with. It cannot be changed after setup.

    The store's name and details, as they should read at the top of every printed record.

    2 · Tax

    This sets how prices are treated on every sale. Only the owner can change it later, and the change is recorded.

    3 · Owner account

    The first account. It can do everything, including creating the rest.

    The PIN unlocks your own open shift at the counter. It is not a second password.

    4 · Recovery code

    This application runs offline, so there is no "forgot password" email. This code is the only way back into the owner account if the password is lost.

    It is shown once, on the last screen of this wizard, and cannot be shown again. Write it down and keep it somewhere away from the computer.

    5 · Backup folder

    This store is on the web. Its backups are kept on Chachi's server, apart from its data, at every shift close and once a day. Download a copy every week from Admin → Backups to keep one yourself.

    Backups run at every shift close and once a day. This folder must be outside the application's own folder — ideally another drive — so a backup survives losing the computer's disk.

    Setup complete

    Write this recovery code down now. It will not be shown again.

    Restore a backup

    This brings the store over from its old computer: products, stock, customers, sales and users, as they were when the backup was taken. Use the newest backup you have — a file named chachipos_backup_…zip, from the old computer's backup folder or the USB stick you copied it to.

    Connect to a store on the web

    This device downloads the store from its web copy and becomes one of its devices: it sells with or without the internet and keeps in step with the web and the store's other devices. Sign in as the store's owner.
