## Full description (4000 max)

```
Chachi POS is a point-of-sale and inventory app for small stores in the Philippines: pharmacies and drugstores, agrivet and feed stores, cafés and restaurants, and neighborhood shops. Sell at the counter, keep stock right, track customer credit (utang), and close each day with a cash count that adds up.

It keeps working without internet. Your records stay on your phone or tablet, and you can also run the same store on the web and on several devices, kept in step.

SELL FAST AT THE COUNTER
• Scan barcodes with a scanner, or search by name or generic name
• Sell by the piece, the box, the bottle or the sack; a box barcode sells a box
• Discounts, including senior citizen and PWD, with the ID recorded
• Cash, GCash, QR Ph and split payments, with change worked out for you
• Print receipts on a network or USB receipt printer, or from the browser
• Returns and voids, with a reason and a manager's approval where it matters

KEEP STOCK RIGHT
• Stock goes down with every sale and up with every delivery
• Low-stock alerts and a reorder list
• Batch numbers and expiry dates, with near-expiry and expired alerts (ideal for medicines and veterinary products)
• Purchase orders, deliveries and short deliveries from your suppliers
• Stock counts of the whole store, a category or by batch, with the variance explained
• Product photos taken with your phone's camera

CUSTOMER CREDIT (UTANG)
• Credit limits and terms per customer
• Collections, statements and an ageing report
• Over-limit sales only with an owner's or manager's approval

SHIFTS AND CASH
• Open a shift with a float, close it with a cash count
• Every peso over or short is shown and explained
• Cash in and cash out during the day, recorded

REPORTS
• Daily sales, payments, by category, by cashier and by product
• Best and slow movers, stock movements and stock value
• Export to CSV and a full backup of the store

FOR CAFÉS AND RESTAURANTS
• Dine-in, take-out and delivery orders, paid now or after the meal
• Kitchen tickets, notes on each item ("less ice") and a service charge
• Items made to order that need no stock

STAFF AND SECURITY
• Owner, manager, cashier and inventory roles, each seeing only what they need
• A PIN to unlock the counter quickly, a password to close the day
• An audit trail of who did what, and when

ONE STORE ON YOUR PHONE, TABLET, PC AND THE WEB
• The counter, stock and customers work offline
• Sales made offline are sent when the internet returns
• Backups to a folder you choose

SUBSCRIPTION
Chachi POS needs an active subscription or a one-time licence. The store owner links the app once with a Google account or an activation key from Chachi's Software. After that the app checks in online at least once every 30 days.

Made in Koronadal City by Chachi's Software Development Service.
Questions or help: ChachiSoftware@gmail.com
Privacy policy: https://pos.chachisoftware.store/privacy
```
